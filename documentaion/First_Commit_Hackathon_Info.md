## Overview

- **Theme:** Build something that solves a real problem — one you deal with yourself, one people around you face daily, or a clunky process nobody has fixed. Open to any sector (healthcare, education, finance, etc.).
- **Format:** Online Thu–Sun, anywhere in India, + optional in-person hack day in Bangalore on Sat, Sept 19 (8 AM–8 PM) at **Polaris School of Technology, Bangalore, Karnataka**.
- **Team size:** 1 to 4 people.
- **Cost:** Free to enter.

## Judging Criteria

1. **Idea and Impact** — Does it solve a real problem, and what changes for people affected?
2. **Built on AWS** — Using an AWS open-source project or AWS service is mandatory to win. (Ship It uses AWS services; Build It uses the open-source stack.)
3. **Learning** — What you learned during the event counts toward the score.
4. **Execution** — Does it work? One solid feature beats five half-finished ones.
5. **Demo video** — Up to 3 minutes; there's no live demo, so the video is what judges see.

Cloud usage/cost/architecture is scored only in Ship It. Best UI is judged on design and usability.

## Rules (full tour rulebook; last updated Sept 16, 2026)

### Who can enter
- University student in India, age 18+.
- One tour-wide registration covers all six hackathons — register once, then check in per event.
- Requires a WeMakeDevs account **and** a verified AWS Builder Center student profile; entry isn't complete without both.
- WeMakeDevs/AWS staff and judges may participate but cannot win prizes.

### Teams
- 1–4 people; same limit online or in person.
- Teammates can be from different universities/cities; each must qualify individually.
- Every member registers with their own account (no captain-registers-all).
- One submission per team; one team per person per hackathon.
- Mixed in-person/online teams are judged the same as fully online/in-person teams.

### What you build
- Build only within the event window (kickoff to deadline). Pre-event learning/practice is fine; pre-built/old projects don't qualify, even if rewritten.
- Open-source libraries, public APIs, boilerplate, and starter templates are allowed — only what you add during the event is judged.
- Must use AWS, and the demo video must show it (mentioning AWS in the writeup alone isn't enough).
- AI coding tools are allowed — list them in the writeup.
- Uncredited/unlicensed third-party work is not allowed.
- You retain rights to your build; entering lets WeMakeDevs/AWS showcase your project and team name.

### Submissions
- Three parts: public repository, demo video (≤3 min), short writeup (problem, build, where AWS fits).
- Submit via the hackathon's submission form, once per team, before the deadline.
- You can submit early and keep editing until the deadline; no submissions or edits after it closes.
- Judges score only what's submitted — no live demos/calls.

### Judging
- Venue and online projects are judged by the same panel on the same criteria.
- Panel decisions are final; scores aren't published or discussed.
- Plagiarism, passing off old projects as new, or repo history mismatched with event dates disqualifies the team.
- Winners announced on the event page and notified by email — keep registration details accurate.

### Fast-track interview at Amazon
- Open to **top projects from Pre-Final Year (2028) and Final Year (2027)** students.
- Up to **10** fast-track interviews per hackathon (a ceiling, not a guarantee — could be fewer or none).
- For a 6-month internship or full-time role at Amazon.
- Eligibility checked against registration details + verified AWS Builder Center profile.
- Winning a prize is neither required nor sufficient for an interview — prizes and interviews are decided separately.
- Amazon/judges/organizers' decision is final; no appeals, no reasons given.
- Opportunity can be modified, paused, or withdrawn at any time.

**Full terms (from Amazon, verbatim except items 10–11):**
1. No guarantee of employment — must clear Amazon's standard interview process.
2. Subject to role availability, headcount, and business needs.
3. Eligibility limited to students in specified degree programs/graduation years meeting Amazon's minimum criteria, with no active disciplinary action.
4. Top-project selection is at the evaluation panel's sole discretion; final and binding.
5. Interview opportunities must be used within the notified timeframe or they lapse.
6. No commitment on visa sponsorship, relocation, or compensation.
7. Amazon may modify, suspend, or withdraw the opportunity anytime without notice.
8. Participating means consenting to project/personal info being shared with Amazon's recruitment team.
9. Non-transferable — applies only to the named individual(s) on the selected team.
10. Winning a track/prize does not qualify you for an interview, and not winning doesn't exclude you.
11. Max 10 interview opportunities per hackathon — an upper limit, not a promise.

### Conduct
- WeMakeDevs Code of Conduct applies online and in person, for the whole event and all channels: https://www.wemakedevs.org/coc
- Harassment, cheating, or abuse of mentors/volunteers/builders ends participation, no appeal.
- At the venue, follow on-site staff instructions.
- Report issues to organizers on the day or by email.

## FAQ Highlights

- **Need AWS knowledge?** No — pre-event workshops run, and Build It needs no AWS account.
- **Need a credit card?** No for Build It. For Ship It, debit cards/RuPay accepted, ~₹2 verification charge.
- **Fully remote participation?** Yes — same theme, judging, and prizes as in-person; no cap on numbers.
- **Does in-person attendance improve chances?** No — it's optional and doesn't add to your score.
- **Certificate?** Everyone who submits gets one; winning team gets an additional certificate.
- **Can I start building early?** Only learning/practice before the clock starts; actual project work must start when the hackathon opens.
