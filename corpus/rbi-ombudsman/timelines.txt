# RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme — Mandatory Timelines and Pre-conditions

## 1. Mandatory 30-Day Pre-requisite Rule
- A complainant CANNOT approach the RBI Ombudsman directly on Day 1.
- Step 1: Complainant must first lodge a formal grievance with the internal Grievance Redressal Officer / Principal Nodal Officer (PNO) of the Regulated Entity (Bank/NBFC/Wallet).
- Step 2: An escalation to the RBI Ombudsman is maintainable ONLY IF:
  1. The Regulated Entity has REJECTED the complaint wholly or partly, OR
  2. The complainant has received NO REPLY from the entity within THIRTY (30) DAYS of lodging the complaint, OR
  3. The complainant is not satisfied with the reply provided by the entity.

## 2. Limitation Period for Filing with RBI Ombudsman
- The complaint must be filed with the RBI Ombudsman within ONE (1) YEAR from the date of receipt of the reply from the Regulated Entity, OR
- Where no reply was received, within ONE (1) YEAR AND THIRTY (30) DAYS from the date of the initial representation to the entity.

## 3. Compensation and Awards
- The Ombudsman may award compensation up to ₹20,00,000 (Rupees Twenty Lakhs) for direct loss incurred by the complainant.
- The Ombudsman may award an additional compensation up to ₹1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh) for loss of time, expenses incurred, harassment, and mental anguish.

## 4. Filing Portal
- RBI Complaint Management System (CMS): https://cms.rbi.org.in
- Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC), Reserve Bank of India, 4th Floor, Sector 17, Chandigarh - 160017.
