# e-Daakhil & Consumer Commission Filing Procedure and Notice Standards

## 1. Formal Legal Notice Structure
Before or alongside an e-Daakhil filing, a formal Legal / Grievance Notice must be issued to the opposite party containing:
1. Sender Details: Full Name, Address, Contact Number, Email.
2. Recipient Details: Company Name, Registered Office Address, Grievance Officer Email.
3. Transaction Facts: Order / Invoice Number, Date of Purchase, Amount Paid, Payment Method (UPI, Card, Net Banking).
4. Specific Deficiency in Service / Unfair Trade Practice: Detailed description of defect, nondelivery, or refusal of refund.
5. Specific Relief Sought: Exact refund amount claimed + interest (typically 18% p.a.) + compensation for harassment and litigation costs.
6. Notice Window: Mandatory 15-day or 30-day cure period for the respondent to comply before statutory filing.

## 2. Online Filing on e-Daakhil Portal
- Platform: https://edaakhil.nic.in
- Mandatory Attachments:
  - Copy of Tax Invoice / Purchase Receipt.
  - Payment transaction receipt / UTR number / bank statement excerpt.
  - Copies of email correspondence, customer support ticket logs, or WhatsApp chat transcripts showing deficiency.
  - Copy of prior legal notice and proof of dispatch/delivery.
  - Complainant identity proof (Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID).
  - Signed verification affidavit.
