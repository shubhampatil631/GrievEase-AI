# TRAI Mandated Two-Tier Escalation Hierarchy and Timelines

## 1. Mandatory Two-Tier Escalation Structure
All telecommunications consumers must follow this statutory escalation hierarchy:

### Tier 1: Complaint Centre / Customer Care (Call Center & Web Portal)
- Customer lodges grievance via toll-free number (198) or operator web portal.
- Service Provider MUST provide a Unique Docket / Complaint Number immediately with an estimated resolution timeline.
- Resolution standard: Billing complaints must be resolved within FOUR (4) WEEKS; service disruptions within 3 DAYS.

### Tier 2: Appellate Authority of the Telecom Service Provider
- If the complaint is NOT resolved within specified time at Tier 1, or the subscriber is dissatisfied with the resolution:
- The subscriber can file an appeal with the Appellate Authority of the respective telecom service provider.
- **Limitation Window:** Appeal must be filed within THIRTY (30) DAYS from the expiry of the Tier 1 resolution time limit.
- Composition of Appellate Authority: An Appellate Authority Secretariat with a 2-member Advisory Committee.
- Appellate Authority must dispose of the appeal within THIRTY-NINE (39) DAYS of filing.

## 2. Escalation Beyond Tier 2
If the grievance remains unresolved after the Appellate Authority's decision:
- Filing a consumer dispute before the District Consumer Commission (DCDRC) via e-Daakhil for deficiency of service.
- National Consumer Helpline (NCH): Call 1915 or file on https://consumerhelpline.gov.in (Integrated Grievance Redressal Mechanism).
- Sanchar Saathi Portal (DoT): https://sancharsaathi.gov.in for lost/stolen device blocking and unauthorized SIM detection.
