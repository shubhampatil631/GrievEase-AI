# Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) — Grievance Categories & Jurisdiction

## 1. Statutory Role & Individual Grievance Jurisdiction
- Important Legal Distinction: Under the TRAI Act 1997, TRAI does NOT directly adjudicate individual consumer complaints.
- Instead, TRAI mandates a strict, binding Two-Tier Grievance Redressal Mechanism that all Telecom Service Providers (TSPs - Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) MUST implement under the Telecom Consumers Protection and Redressal of Grievances Regulations.
- Unresolved issues beyond the Appellate Authority escalate to the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT) or the Consumer Commission (DCDRC/e-Daakhil).

## 2. Covered Grievance Categories
- **Billing & Charging Discrepancies:** Incorrect tariff application, unauthorized value-added service (VAS) activation, wrong data meter charges, non-refund of security deposit.
- **Service Quality & Disconnection:** Network blackouts, persistent call drops, broadband fiber outage exceeding SLA without credit, arbitrary disconnection of SIM.
- **Mobile Number Portability (MNP):** Rejection of Unique Porting Code (UPC), delay in porting beyond statutory window (2 to 4 working days).
- **Unsolicited Commercial Communications (UCC / DND):** Spam calls and SMS violations under TCCCPR 2018.
- **SIM Swap / Fraudulent Deactivation:** Unauthorized SIM swap leading to financial fraud.
